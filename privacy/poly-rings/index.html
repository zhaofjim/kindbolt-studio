About this policy

This policy applies to Poly Rings by Kindbolt Studio, Android package com.kindbolt.apps.poly_rings. Effective November 6, 2026, for version 1.0.0. Build 4 added advertisements; build 5 added a local age-group choice before advertising is enabled. Earlier builds without ads do not request advertisements. Contact: kindboltstudio@163.com.

Local game data

Puzzle progress, rotations, move history, completed levels, hint usage and preferences are saved in private app storage. The game does not upload your puzzle saves to the developer and has no account, chat, purchases or cloud save. Puzzles and artwork are bundled and remain playable offline.

Advertising and network access

The Android app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) for interstitial advertisements between levels and optional rewarded advertisements for an extra hint. Levels 1–10 do not request or display ads. Ad requests start only after entering Level 11 or later. Ads require a network connection. If an interstitial cannot load, the next level remains accessible. Rewarded ads are optional and a hint is granted only when Google confirms the reward.

Data processed by Google

When advertising services are used, Google may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), ad and app interactions, diagnostic information and applicable device or app identifiers for ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or shared storage permissions. Removing advertising ID access does not mean that all advertising-related data collection stops. Google handles its data under its own policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

All-age audience and privacy choices

This version applies child-directed ad treatment and the G (general audience) maximum ad content rating to every player, requests non-personalized ads, and removes Android advertising ID and AdServices permissions. Before advertising is first enabled, build 5 asks for an age group with no preselected answer and a “Prefer not to say” option. The age group is stored only on the device and is not sent as a custom field to the advertising provider. All choices receive the same child-directed ad restrictions. No date of birth is requested. Google User Messaging Platform manages applicable privacy requirements; if it requires a privacy options entry, it appears in Settings → More → Ad privacy choices. These settings do not remove the network and diagnostic processing described above.

Retention and deletion

Game saves remain until you erase progress, clear Android app data or uninstall. Settings → More → Erase progress deletes puzzle progress and hint usage while retaining preferences and the age-group choice; native ad frequency records and Google privacy choices are retained separately to avoid repeated ads and preserve choices. Clearing Android app data or uninstalling removes local app records. It does not delete data already processed by Google; consult Google privacy controls and retention policies. The developer cannot restore saves because we do not receive them. Android cloud backup and app-data transfer are disabled.

Support and updates

If you email kindboltstudio@163.com, we use your address and message to answer your request and necessary follow-up, retaining them only as needed unless legally required. You can contact us to request deletion of support correspondence. Do not send passwords or unnecessary information about children. Android, Google Play and your email provider have their own policies. We update this policy when our data practices change.
