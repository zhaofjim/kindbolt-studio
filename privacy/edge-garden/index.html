About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to Edge Garden (棱间花园) versions 1.0.0, 1.1.0, 1.2.0, 1.3.0, 1.3.1, 1.4.0 and 1.5.0 for Android, package com.kindbolt.apps.edge_garden, published by Kindbolt Studio. Effective date: October 22, 2026. Privacy and support contact: kindboltstudio@163.com.

No data collection or sharing through the game

Edge Garden does not collect or transmit personal information or gameplay data to the developer or third parties. It contains no accounts, advertisements, analytics SDKs, purchases, chat or user-generated content. It does not request names, email addresses, location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or advertising identifiers.

Information stored on your device

The game stores puzzle orientations, move history, completed gardens, best move counts, hint counts and preferences such as language, sound, touch feedback and reduced motion in private app storage. This supports continued play and is never sent to a server. There is no cloud synchronization. Android cloud backup and app-data device transfer are disabled in the app configuration.

Offline operation and device features

Puzzles, artwork and sound synthesis are bundled with the app. No network connection or sign-in is required. The Android app requests no permissions, including Internet, advertising ID, storage, microphone or location permissions. Optional touch feedback uses the Android view haptic feedback API, which requires no VIBRATE permission and respects device settings. The game uses Android System WebView for its bundled local content. The appassets.androidplatform.net base URL identifies a local document origin, not a remote game service. App network requests and external navigation are blocked.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains until you erase it through Settings → Erase progress, clear the app storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. In-game erasure removes puzzle progress, completion records and best scores while preserving your chosen settings. Android storage clearing or uninstalling removes all local app data. There is no account or server-side game data to delete. The developer cannot restore local saves because we never receive them.

Children and families

Edge Garden is a nonviolent spatial puzzle game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other players and has no advertising or social features. Parents and guardians may contact the developer with privacy questions.

Support correspondence and platform services

If you voluntarily email kindboltstudio@163.com, the email address and information you provide are used to answer your request and reasonable follow-up. Do not send passwords, sensitive details or unnecessary information about children. Support messages are not collected by the game. They are retained only as needed to resolve your request and follow-up, unless retention is legally required; email the same address to request deletion. Your email provider, Android, device vendor and Google Play have their own privacy policies. Their platform services are outside this game’s local data processing.

Security, changes and contact

The game keeps data within private Android app storage and does not claim to apply its own encryption to saves. Device security depends on Android and your device settings. We will update this policy and store disclosures if future versions change the data practices described here. Privacy and support contact: kindboltstudio@163.com.
