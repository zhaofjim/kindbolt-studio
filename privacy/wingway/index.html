About this policy This Privacy Policy applies to Wingway version 1.0.0 for Android (package com.kindbolt.apps.wingway), published by the independent developer of Wingway. Privacy and support contact: 1297273953@qq.com. Last updated: October 20, 2026.

No data collection or sharing through the game Wingway does not transmit personal information or gameplay data to the developer or third parties. The game has no account registration, advertisements, analytics SDKs, purchase system, chat or user-generated content. It does not request your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or advertising identifier.

Information stored on your device Wingway stores game progress, scores, unfinished puzzles, challenge records and preferences such as language, sound, vibration and display settings in its private app storage on your device. This information supports gameplay and is not sent to a server. The game does not provide cloud backup or synchronization. The Android app disables automatic app backup.

Offline operation and permissions Puzzles, artwork and prerecorded voices are bundled with the game. No live speech service is contacted and no voice recording is made. The app does not request the Internet permission. It uses the normal VIBRATE permission for optional touch feedback; vibration can be turned off in settings. The app uses Android System WebView to display bundled local content. Its appassets.androidplatform.net address identifies local app assets, not a remote game service.

Retention and deletion Local game data remains until you clear Wingway app storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. These actions permanently remove local progress and scores. We cannot restore this data because we do not receive or keep a copy. There is no account to delete and no server-side game data deletion request is needed.

Children and families Wingway is designed as a nonviolent puzzle game for a broad audience, including children who can understand its spatial rules. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other players, and has no advertising or social features. Parents and guardians may contact the developer with privacy questions.

Support correspondence and platform services If you voluntarily email the developer, your email address and the information you choose to include are used to answer your request. Do not send passwords, sensitive personal details or unnecessary information about children. Support messages are not collected by the game. They are kept only as needed to resolve the request and reasonable follow-up, unless retention is required by law; contact the same address to request deletion. Your email provider, Android and Google Play operate under their own policies. This policy describes the developer’s handling of Wingway game data and does not replace those policies.

Security, changes and contact Game data is kept within Android app storage; Wingway does not claim to encrypt local saves itself. Device security is managed by Android and your device settings. If future versions add services that change data practices, this policy and the store disclosures will be updated. Questions about this policy or support messages: 1297273953@qq.com.
